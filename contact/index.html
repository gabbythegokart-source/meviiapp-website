Quick answers

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open My mevii, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete account. It erases everything right away. You can also email us and we’ll do it for you.

How do I report or block someone?

On a review, tap the … button, then Report or Block. For a person, go to My mevii, then Friends, and tap Options on a friend or Report or block on a request.

I didn’t get my confirmation email.

Check your spam or junk folder for an email from mevii. Still nothing after a few minutes? Email us with the address you signed up with.

Why isn’t something showing on my streaming services?

mevii shows what’s streaming in the U.S., using data from JustWatch through TMDB. Make sure your services are picked in My mevii. Availability changes often, so a title can come and go.

Is mevii free?

Yes. There are no ads and we don’t sell your data.
