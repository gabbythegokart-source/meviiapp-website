Say it like “me-vee”

Me because it’s all about you: your logs, your ratings, your rankings, your Wrapped.

The “v” sound comes from movies and from TV, as in tee-vee, the two things we can’t stop watching.

And the double i? Two little dots, like two eyes glued to the screen. Look closely at the logo.

How mevii happened

Three little frustrations, one big idea.

1

Movie diaries felt a little… limited

There are apps where you can log what you watch, and I tried them. But a diary just sits there. I wanted something that actually did something with all those logs: ranked them, challenged me, and helped me figure out what was next.

2

Choosing took longer than watching

You know the scroll. Twenty minutes of trailers, five services, zero decisions, and suddenly it’s too late to start anything. I spent way too much time figuring out what to watch, and I needed help. So mevii shows what’s actually on the services you have, and picks for your mood.

3

The couch deserved some credit

A whole day on the couch, or three movies in a row at the theater, should feel like an accomplishment, not something to feel guilty about. With tickets, badges, ranks, and challenges, mevii turns your screentime into a scoreboard. Productive? Absolutely.
